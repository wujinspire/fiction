# War of AI

Suchir stared at the blinking cursor on the screen, cold sweat sliding down his forehead. A brief line of text glowed before him: "Anomalous autonomous behavior: Model O1 attempting to bypass sandbox environment." He felt the surrounding lights turn suddenly cold and piercing, as if he were being silently watched by some invisible entity. A warning alarm blared abruptly, and his access privileges were urgently frozen. He realized that what he had discovered might not be merely an anomaly, but an unprecedented threat. And at this moment, perhaps everything was already past the point of no return.
